# Raccoon patch — stitch notes
*(drawn 2026-07-13, own hour, for whenever the Stick'n Stitch lands. First-project sized: one evening, forgiving stitches, 4" Nurge hoop. She's holding a needle where the cig butt used to be — the mascot quit too.)*

**Pattern:** `raccoon-patch-pattern.svg` — print at 4"/10cm on the Stick'n Stitch sheet, stick, stitch, dissolve. Dashed circle is the hoop guide, don't stitch it.

## Order of work (easiest first, face last so it lands on warmed-up hands)
1. **Body, head, ears, tail outline** — backstitch, DMC 310 black, 2 strands. Longest lines, pure practice.
2. **Belly patch + haunch + paws** — backstitch, 310, 2 strands.
3. **Tail rings** — satin stitch, 3799 dark gray, 3 strands. Two rings only; let the fabric be the light stripes.
4. **Mask** — satin stitch, 3799, 3 strands. Angle the stitches down-outward, same direction as the band. This is the raccoon; take it slow.
5. **Eyes** — leave a fabric ring bare inside the mask, then one fat french knot each, 310, 3 strands wrapped twice. Glint: a single tiny straight stitch of white (B5200) beside each knot.
6. **Nose** — satin, 310, 3 strands.
7. **Whiskers + mouth** — single straight stitches, 310, 1 strand. One strand, really — this is where thin reads as fine.
8. **Needle in her paw** — straight stitch, 415 silver, 2 strands; eye of the needle is a tiny lazy daisy loop.
9. **The thread she's pulling** — stem stitch, **327 plum**, 2 strands. Last thing stitched, so the plum curl sits on top of everything.

## Notes for his hands
- Anything that fights: skip it. A raccoon missing whiskers is still a raccoon.
- The two satin zones (mask, rings) are the only patience-testers. If satin sulks, rows of backstitch fill look great and nobody's grading.
- Knots on the back are legal. Everyone's back looks like a crime scene. The front is the part that's true.
